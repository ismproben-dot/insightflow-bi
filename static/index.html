01 / DEFINITIONS

Know your metrics

Revenue
Σ quantity × unit price
Gross profit
Σ quantity × (unit price − unit cost)
Gross margin
Gross profit ÷ revenue × 100
Average order value
Revenue ÷ distinct order IDs

Gross profit excludes shipping, tax, discounts and overhead. This is not net profit.

02 / COMPARISON

A fair period comparison

Choose both a start and an end date to compare with the immediately preceding period of the same number of calendar days.

Region and category filters remain applied. If previous revenue is zero, growth is shown as unavailable.

Category filters measure only matching order lines. Missing months are not forecast or imputed.

03 / QUALITY

Validate, then import

The import preview checks required fields, calendar dates, prices, quantities and duplicate lines. Invalid lines are listed with their CSV record number.

Orders must have a consistent date and region. You explicitly choose whether to import the valid rows when errors exist.

04 / SCOPE

One currency per dataset

Choose MAD, EUR or USD during import. No exchange-rate conversion is performed. All financial values are stored in integer minor units.

Local portfolio app. No user accounts, forecasting, payment integration or public hosting included.
